this formalisation run). The note is based on Smooth Pudding, 「炭化水素を列挙してみた

digest in `docs/math/degree_labeling.md`. Its original connectedness and degree-at-most-4
motivation is not a hypothesis of either target theorem.

Chemical Structures — A Technique Developed at Chemical Abstracts Service,” *Journal of
